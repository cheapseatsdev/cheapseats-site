Popcorn (popcorn.zone) is the MIT-licensed, self-hosted AI visibility tracker. It asks Claude and OpenAI models your buyers' questions, grades whether each answer recommends your brand, merely mentions it or leaves it out, and ends every run with a ranked list of fixes. These pages compare it, one product at a time, with the hosted tools people most often weigh it against.

Each comparison uses only Popcorn's public facts and what each vendor says on its own pages, as of October 2026, and each says plainly when the other product is the better choice. The short version: Popcorn is free and open source, runs on your laptop or on a host you pay for, keeps your prompts in your own git repo, and asks only Claude and OpenAI models. If you need Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Google AI Overviews or AI Mode, a hosted tool covers them and Popcorn doesn't.

For six tools side by side, see [AI visibility tools compared](/blog/ai-visibility-tools-compared/).
